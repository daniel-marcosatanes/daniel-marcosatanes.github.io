## 🧬 The Mission
Chemistry is often seen as static structures on paper. My goal is to bring them to life.
I am a **PhD Researcher** passionate about understanding *how* and *why* reactions happen. By combining experimental synthesis (getting my hands dirty) with DFT calculations (understanding the invisible), I design precise catalytic tools for C–H functionalization.

### 🗺️ The Journey So Far
*   **📍 Present:** Decoding mechanisms at **CiQUS** (Santiago de Compostela) as a Xunta de Galicia Fellow.
*   **🎓 PhD Era:** Defended *Cum Laude* (2025) under Prof. Mascareñas.
*   **🌍 Global Science:**
    *   Exploring high-throughput experimentation at **Hartwig Lab** (UC Berkeley) as a *Fulbright Scholar*.
    *   Modeling enzymes at **Jiménez-Osés Lab** (CIC bioGUNE).
*   **🔮 Next Stop (2026):** Chasing photons and radicals at the **Melchiorre Group** (University of Bologna).

## 🔭 Visualizing the Invisible
One of the core components of this portfolio is making complex data accessible.

### [DMA Thesis Viewer 🧊](https://danielmarcosatanes.github.io/DMA_thesis-viewer/)
A specialized web tool built to visualize the transition states and molecular intermediates of my doctoral thesis. Because static PDFs don't do justice to dynamic chemistry.
*   **Tech:** JSmol + HTML5.
*   **Goal:** Open Science & Reproducibility.

## ⚙️ Under the Hood
This website is built with **Open Source** tools, embracing the philosophy of transparent research.

*   **Engine:** [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) (Static Site Generator).
*   **Hosting:** GitHub Pages (CD/CI).
*   **Theme:** A customized version of [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio), tailored for academic storytelling.
*   **Bibliography:** Automated via BibTeX integration.
