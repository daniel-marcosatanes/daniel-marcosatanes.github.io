Bridging the gap between synthetic organometallic chemistry and biological environments is a key challenge for future therapeutics and biocatalysis.

### Ruthenium in Aqueous Media
I contributed to a collaborative study focusing on **Ruthenium-catalyzed alkene-alkyne couplings** in biologically relevant media (*Chem. Sci.* 2023).
*   **The Challenge:** Most organometallic catalysts are deactivated by water or cellular components.
*   **The Result:** We demonstrated a robust Ru(II) system capable of operating in aqueous conditions and in the presence of biomolecules.
