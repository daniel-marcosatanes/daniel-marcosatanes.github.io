        <h3>From Waste to Wealth</h3>
        <p>Plastic waste is not just an environmental crisis; it is an untapped carbon resource. While mechanical recycling degrades material properties, <strong>chemical upcycling</strong> offers a path to value.</p>
        <p>In the Hartwig Lab (UC Berkeley), we asked a bold question: <em>Can we use organometallic catalysis to precision-edit a polymer chain?</em></p>
        <p>By replacing reactive C–Cl bonds with organoboron motifs, we successfully transformed brittle waste PVC into a <strong>tunable adhesive material</strong>. This work proves that fundamental reactivity principles can solve macroscopic challenges.</p>
