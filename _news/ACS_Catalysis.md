Our work titled *"Bis-CF3-bipyridine Ligands for the Iridium-Catalyzed Borylation of N-Methylamides"* has been accepted in **ACS Catalysis**. We demonstrate for the first time the transfer of dispersion-controlled selectivity to aliphatic systems.
